## 📖 Description

Hunt down **Mimics** — mobs disguised with a chest on their head — and kill them to claim a trinket.

Trinkets are passive: just carry one in your inventory and it works. Each one reacts to something you do — taking a hit, landing a kill, shooting a bow, sneaking, falling — and most trigger on a chance roll, so they feel like lucky procs rather than guaranteed effects.

**35 trinkets**, each in **4 rarity tiers** — **140 variants** in total. Higher tiers roll more often, last longer, or hit harder:

| Tier | Drop chance |
|:--|:--|
| Common | 55% |
| Uncommon | 28% |
| Rare | 14% |
| Epic | 3% |

They come in three flavors:

- **⚔️ Offensive** — punish whatever you hit. Set enemies on fire, poison or wither them, summon evoker fangs, trap them in cobwebs, or call down a lightning bolt.
- **🛡️ Defensive** — keep you alive. Resistance and regeneration when you get hurt, a ring of fangs erupting around you, slow falling mid-drop, or a second chance at death itself.
- **🧪 Utility** — everything else. Night vision and invisibility on sneak, conduit power underwater, haste while mining, piercing arrows, and a chance to get your ender pearls, wind charges or XP bottles back after using them.

Mix and match — carry as many as you want at once.

## Config

Run `/function trinkery:config` to open the settings menu.

- **Spawn Mimic**: whether mimics can spawn naturally. Enabled by default.
- **Kill all mimics**: remove every mimic currently in the world.
- **Reset**: restore every option to its default.
